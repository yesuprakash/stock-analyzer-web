🎯 Near Entry Screen

RSI, MA20, MACD, volume ratio (minimum 1.2), reward/risk, gap (maximum 1.5%), and ATR checks determine actionability. Reward/risk is recalculated from the last price. The analyzer probability score is not an actionability filter. Prices come from Yahoo Finance daily bars and are not guaranteed live or complete during market hours.
