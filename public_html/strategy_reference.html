Screen Logic Reference

This page explains the business rules used to shortlist stocks. A screen result is a research aid, not a promise of performance or a recommendation to trade. Prices are sourced from daily market data and may be delayed or incomplete during market hours.

Near Entry

Near Entry watches the latest saved prediction for each active stock and finds cases where the latest market price is close to that prediction's entry price.

1. Near-entry shortlist

  • The latest price must be within the selected percentage tolerance of the saved entry price. The default is within 2% either above or below entry.
  • The percentage difference is measured relative to the entry price. The screen does not require price to approach entry from only one direction.
  • Stocks outside this price range are not shown. With “Show Only Actionable” off, stocks inside the range can still appear even when they fail one or more actionability checks.

2. Actionability checks

Default thresholds and requirements:

  • The prediction and price data must each be no more than 3 calendar days old.
  • At least 50 daily closing prices must be available.
  • The saved prediction signal must be Strong Buy, Buy, or Bullish.
  • RSI must be at least 45, price must be above its 20-day moving average, and MACD histogram must be non-negative.
  • Current volume must be at least 1.2 times its recent five-session average.
  • Reward-to-risk is recalculated from the latest price and the prediction's target and stop. Both potential reward and risk must be positive, and the ratio must be at least 1.5.
  • The opening price gap from the previous close must be no more than 1.5% in either direction.
  • Average True Range (ATR) must be no more than 5% of the latest price.

RSI, moving-average, and MACD checks can be switched off individually. Volume, reward-to-risk, gap, volatility, signal, age, and minimum-history checks remain required for an actionable result. The prediction probability score is displayed as information and does not decide actionability.

“Show Only Actionable” filters the near-entry shortlist to stocks that pass every enabled actionability check. Otherwise, qualifying near-entry stocks are shown with their failed checks.

Market Correction

Market Correction looks for active stocks whose latest close has fallen into a selected range below a recent high, while checking momentum, volatility, and the available target/stop levels. It evaluates the most recent saved prediction for each active stock.

1. Correction range

  • The default measurement window is 30 trading sessions; other available windows range from 5 to 60 sessions.
  • The correction percentage compares the latest close with the highest closing price in that window. The default accepted range is from 20% below to 8% below that high.
  • The “Drawdown from Window High” value compares the latest close with the highest daily high in the same window. It is descriptive and is not the correction-range filter.

2. Strategy thresholds

  • RSI must be at least 40.
  • MACD histogram must be non-negative.
  • ATR must be no more than 5.5% of the latest price.
  • Reward-to-risk uses the latest price and the saved prediction's target and stop. Potential reward and risk must both be positive, and the ratio must be at least 1.8.
  • The prediction and price data must each be no more than 5 calendar days old.

The prediction signal is shown in the results but is not itself a pass/fail threshold on this screen. Target and stop levels come from the latest saved prediction.

“Skip strategy thresholds” bypasses the correction, RSI, MACD, ATR, and reward-to-risk thresholds. It does not bypass the need for a recent prediction, fresh market data, or enough valid price history to calculate the selected window.

Both screens depend on historical daily prices and saved prediction levels. Intraday prices, execution slippage, liquidity, and future market events can differ from what the screens show.
